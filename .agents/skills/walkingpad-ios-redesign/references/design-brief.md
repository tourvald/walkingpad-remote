# Design brief contract

Keep the brief decision-oriented and small:

1. Screen/flow, primary user, and the single problem being solved.
2. Current-state evidence and at most 8 prioritized audit findings.
3. Required states: normal, loading, empty, error, disabled, accessibility, and relevant size classes.
4. Existing project patterns/tokens to preserve.
5. Exactly 2 concepts maximum, each with benefit, tradeoff, and safety/scope fit.
6. PM-selected direction and rejected alternative rationale.
7. Exact writable paths, mock inputs, acceptance criteria, and non-goals.

Do not put generic design guidance, speculative features, production data, or repeated chat history in the brief.
